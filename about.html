How we work

Calculate, test, log

Soap making is chemistry with a small margin. Running every recipe through a calculator takes two minutes and prevents lye-heavy bars, and knowing which fragrances accelerate prevents most ruined batches.

Candles are pure testing. The wick chart is a starting point, not an answer, and the only way to know a combination works is to burn it repeatedly and write down what happened.

Both reward records. Makers who log every batch improve steadily; makers who rely on memory repeat the same faults and never work out why.

Our principles

Three beliefs behind the method

PPE every time

Goggles and gloves, ventilation, and never water into lye.

Log every batch

The log is what turns accidents into knowledge.

Test before selling

Burn tests and labels come before money changes hands.
